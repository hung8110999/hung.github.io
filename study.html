How I Study

My Study Method

🧠

Active Recall

Testing myself on concepts rather than passively re-reading. This forces deeper understanding.

🔄

Spaced Repetition

Reviewing material at increasing intervals to move knowledge into long-term memory.

Teach to Learn

Writing notes as if teaching someone else. If I can explain it simply, I truly understand it.
