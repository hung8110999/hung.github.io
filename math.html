Mathematics

My math study notes - from foundational concepts to advanced topics. Based on two main books: Mathematics for Machine Learning by Marc Peter Deisenroth, A. Aldo Faisal, Cheng Soon Ong and the book "Toán học chân phương" by Vũ Phính.

Algebra
Algebra: The foundation of all higher mathematics, dealing with symbols and the rules for manipulating those symbols.
Calculus
Calculus: The mathematical study of continuous change, divided into differential calculus and integral calculus.
Linear Algebra
Linear Algebra: The branch of mathematics concerning linear equations, matrices, and vector spaces. Essential for ML.
Statistics
Probability & Statistics: The mathematical framework for quantifying uncertainty and analyzing data.
Discrete Math
Discrete Mathematics: The study of mathematical structures that are fundamentally discrete rather than continuous. Includes graphs, integers, and logic.
