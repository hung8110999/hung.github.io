## Quadratic Formula

The solutions to $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ are:

$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$

## Integral Example

$$
\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \left[ \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^1 = \frac{1}{3}
$$

You can also use inline notation like $\sum_{i=1}^n i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$.
